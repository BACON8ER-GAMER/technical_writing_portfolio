About Nate

Nate Alberda is a technical writer based in the Greater Seattle area. He discovered a passion for writing in college and pursued a Professional Technical Writing Certificate from the University of Washington. Through portfolio projects in the program and an interest in technology, he enjoys writing clear, concise documentation and building websites using tools such as Visual Studio Code, Git, and GitHub.

To help others and sharpen his technical writing skills, Nate volunteers to assist with editing and refining documentation on public GitHub repositories. He wants to create technical content that is approachable and usable to improve the quality of people’s lives.

Nate also holds a Bachelor of Arts in Music Production from Seattle Pacific University. Outside of writing, he enjoys playing video games, lifting weights, reading, and playing the piano.
